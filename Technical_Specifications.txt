What are Technical Specifications?

A technical specification (often called a "Tech Spec") describes how the system is implemented. 
It answers questions like:

What technologies were used?
How is the system structured/Architecture?
How do the services communicate?
What infrastructure is required?
What APIs exist?
What databases are used?
How is security handled?
How is the system deployed?

Think of it as the engineering manual for your project.
